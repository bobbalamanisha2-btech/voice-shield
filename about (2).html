About VoiceShield AI

AI-assisted detection and prevention of voice-cloning impersonation attacks.

The Problem

Generative AI can synthesize convincing speech. Attackers may use cloned voices to impersonate family members, employees or executives and pressure victims into unsafe actions.

Our Solution

VoiceShield extracts acoustic features, evaluates them with a trained classifier when available, and turns the result into a risk score and security recommendation.

Architecture

🎙 Audio Input→⚙ Preprocessing→🧠 AI Detector→🛡 Verify / Alert / Block

Important Limitation

This starter project has a demo fallback when a trained model is absent. For a real SIH submission, train and evaluate on a representative real-vs-synthetic speech dataset and report accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score and false-positive/negative rates.
